How to play

  1. Order — greet the customer at the counter and take their ticket. Everyone waiting loses patience, so keep the line moving.
  2. Build — tap the sauce and cheese buttons to hit the right amount, then drag toppings from the bins. Watch for half-and-half orders.
  3. Bake — slide the pie into an oven slot. Pull it at the doneness the ticket asks for. Ovens keep cooking while you work elsewhere.
  4. Cut — the cutter sweeps around the pie. Click to cut when it lines up with the guides. Right slices, clean angles.
  5. Serve — three meters, one tip. Best day score goes on the weekly ladder.
