Endless yellow. Familiar grin. You are not lost, you are relocated.

How to play

  1. Look — move the mouse (click the hallway to lock the pointer). Move — WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to sprint (drains stamina).
  2. Find all grin fragments 🧌 glowing in the halls. The counter top-left tracks how many you've found.
  3. The minimap (top-left) fills in as you explore — it only shows halls you've actually walked.
  4. Once every fragment is found, the exit door lights up. Walk through it to drop to the next level — the halls get bigger and stranger the deeper you go.
  5. Something else walks these halls. If it spots you down a corridor, it will chase — and it's faster than you can walk. Teal-lit alcoves are hiding spots: stand in one and hold E to duck out of sight until it loses interest.
  6. Getting caught sends you back to the start of the current level. Press Esc any time to release the pointer lock and pause.
