How to play

  1. You are the fry cook. The screen is what you see. Move your mouse to look around, and turn between the griddle, the counter, and the order window with ← → (or the edge arrows, or keys 1 / 2 / 3).
  2. Griddle — tap the tub to lay a patty. Each patty cooks the side facing DOWN. Tap a patty to flip it; hit the green band on both sides, then plate it. Burnt is forever.
  3. Counter — pin a ticket, then tap bins in the ticket's exact stack order. Patties come from your plate rack. Wrong order costs you.
  4. Window — tickets ding through the pass. Finished Trolly Patty? Ring the bell. Faster service, bigger tips.
  5. Fries, rings, drinks unlock as shifts go on — drop a basket on the griddle and pull it golden, or hold the soda machine and let go in the sweet spot.
  6. Watch the banner. Mr. Grabs walks the floor (don't waste food while he's watching), a rush hour floods the window, Gremlin sneaks toward the safe (tap him 3x), and the health inspector shows up (serve nothing burnt, wipe the counter). Close out the shift with a quick scrub-mop-flip.
  7. Remember: patties keep cooking while your back is turned. That's the job.
